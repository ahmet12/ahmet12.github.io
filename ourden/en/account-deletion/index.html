Delete your Ourden account

If you cannot access the app, email us from your registered address with “Ourden account deletion request” in the subject. Tell us you want to delete your Ourden account and its personal data.

kylindravia@gmail.com

If you no longer have access to your registered address, email the same mailbox to ask how to verify your identity. Leave out passwords, sign-in codes and identity document images. We will reply to verify your request and tell you the outcome.

Delete in the app

You can also delete it in the app: Settings › Account and sign-in › Delete account.

When you delete your account

When we verify and accept your deletion, your sessions close. Your account and personal records normally enter permanent deletion at the end of a 30-day closed period. A home linked only to your account closes. Shared chores, meals and history remain in homes that others use. We remove your profile name, and references to you show “Former member”.

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription; you manage it in the store.
