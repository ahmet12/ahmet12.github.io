Subscription model

Ourden is free to download and invited members join a home free of charge. Each member signs in with their own account. One person pays for one home, and all its members share that plan on iOS and Android.

When the store screen offers **Household + AI**, you can choose between two plans for your home.

- **Household Annual** includes chores, planning, meals and shopping. Eligible store accounts can try it free for 7 days. - **Household + AI Monthly or Annual** includes those features and AI suggestions in one subscription. Each member chooses whether to allow their data to be sent for AI. One purchase covers the home.

The purchase screen shows the store’s current price, billing period and any trial before you confirm payment. To change plans, use the store account that paid for the home. The store shows when the change takes effect and what you will pay. If you switch from Household + AI to Household, paid AI access continues through the end of the period. Accepted chores, dishes and lists stay in the home when AI access ends.

If you bought Household and AI separately in an older version, you will see two renewals in the store. Manage each in the account used to purchase it. Cancelling Household alone leaves the separate AI renewal in place. The old AI subscription requires an active Household subscription under its original terms. To avoid paying twice, wait until your separate AI period ends before buying Household + AI. Use Restore Purchases in the app to recover existing access.

Rights you previously bought or received for free are preserved under their original terms. Existing core features, app updates and accessibility options stay in Household. Dark mode and text size are free. Use an Ourden invitation to add members. App Store Family Sharing purchases do not grant access to an Ourden household. The store’s standard licence and service terms also apply when you obtain the app.
