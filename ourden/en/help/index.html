Support and safe contact

Ourden is published by Ahmet Kılıç (Kylindravia). Email kylindravia@gmail.com for support, privacy or security requests. Tell us which screen has the problem, what you expected, your app version and whether you use iOS or Android. Hide names, email addresses and household content before attaching a screenshot.

Do not send your password, one-time code, invitation link or payment details. We do not need them for support.

Invitations and joining

Someone in the home sends you an invitation link or code. Joining is free; you do not need to buy the household subscription. If the link does not open, paste it into “I’m joining with an invitation” in Ourden or enter the code. Ask for a new invitation if it has expired or is invalid. An administrator approves your request to join.

Sign-in, passwords and email codes

Sign in with Apple, Google, an email code or a password you previously set. Use the email address registered with Ourden. If you used Apple’s Hide My Email, it may be a private relay address; check your account settings.

If a code has not arrived, check the address and spam folder. Request another when the button becomes available, and enter it only on the Ourden verification screen. A new device may require a code even with the correct password. Use the app’s password reset flow if needed. If Google or Apple sign-in is unavailable, try the email route for the same account.

Subscriptions, cancellation and restoring purchases

If you are invited to a home, join with your own account. One person pays for the home. When the purchase screen offers Household + AI, you can choose Household Annual, Household + AI Monthly or Household + AI Annual. Members share the plan on iOS and Android. Each member controls their own AI sharing permission.

To change plans, cancel or check an upcoming charge, open subscriptions in the store account that paid. When you switch from Household + AI to Household, paid AI access continues to the end of the period. If you have two subscriptions from an older version, check both renewals. Cancelling Household alone leaves the separate AI renewal in place. Deleting an account leaves store renewals active. Manage your subscriptions in the App Store or Google Play.

If your purchase is missing, sign in to the same store account and choose **Restore Purchases** on Ourden’s subscription screen. Contact support before buying again if the home still shows no access.

When the household plan ends, you can read the home and export its data. Chores, dishes and lists you already accepted stay in the home when AI access ends.

AI sharing permission

AI is optional. Before the first request, we explain what will be shared with OpenAI and ask permission. You can use the core household features without AI if you decline. Withdraw permission in Settings › AI suggestions; another request needs permission again. A provider call that already started cannot be withdrawn retroactively. See the Privacy page.

Notifications and offline syncing

If notifications do not arrive, check the device’s Ourden notification permission, in-app preferences and quiet hours. Personal reminders are scheduled on your device; household notifications also depend on a connection and provider delivery.

The shared list works without a connection. Pending changes sync when you reconnect. “Waiting to sync” means the server has not confirmed the action. Try syncing pending changes before uninstalling the app or switching accounts.

Exporting data and deleting an account

Request an export in your home’s settings, then download it in the app when ready. The download file is temporary; you lose access when you delete your account. A household export does not replace every kind of personal data request.

In the app, go to Settings › Account and sign-in › Delete account. Review what will be deleted and what will remain in a shared home, then confirm. If you do not have the app, open the account deletion page. From your registered email address, send us a message saying “Please delete my Ourden account.” If you can no longer access that address, contact us from another address and explain. We verify that the account is yours and that you requested deletion, then let you know the outcome by email.

If other account holders share a home where you are the last administrator, first transfer that role or close the home. A home where yours is the only account closes. If a recent email change can still be reversed, deletion can finish once that period ends. The privacy notice explains retention periods and which records remain in a shared home. To end a store subscription too, cancel it separately in the store where you bought it.

Usage measurement preference

If your app shows Usage measurement in Settings, it starts off. You can choose to share information about which steps in household setup cause difficulty and which features are used. You can turn it off again in the same place. Your choice does not affect your household plan.

You can find this setting in version 1.2.5 (build 1351). It is unavailable in the earlier 1.2.5 build (1303) and previous releases. To stop measurement, send an objection from your registered email address. A previously accepted objection stays in effect.

Personal data requests

To ask for access, correction or deletion, start a KVKK request by email. For example, you can ask which data Ourden processes about your account, for what purposes, and who receives it abroad. To object to usage metrics, you can ask us to stop collecting new usage events for your account.

Send formal email requests in Turkish from your registered address. The requests section of the privacy notice lists the required details, including your name and address for service. You can also contact us about a profile if you have no account. Email us for the address for written applications. Leave identity documents and household content out of your first support message. We respond within 30 days.

A message to share before adding a household profile

“We use Ourden to plan chores and meals. If I add a profile for you, authorised members of our home will see the name or nickname we choose, the avatar and the planning details we add. The profile is optional. The data controller is Ahmet Kılıç (Kylindravia). You can read about the data and your rights at https://ahmet12.github.io/ourden/en/privacy/. You can ask for correction or deletion at any time by emailing kylindravia@gmail.com.”

For a child, give this information to the child’s legal representative. Explain that you can use a nickname, an age band and the child’s participation in meal planning. Tell the person which details you actually intend to enter.

Separate permission for a household profile

After providing the notice, ask separately, “May I use those details to add a profile for our household’s shared planning?” Add the profile only after the adult or the child’s legal representative gives you a clear yes. They are free to decline or to leave the question unanswered. Permission for a profile does not cover optional AI processing.
