Restore your vault on another device

From Vault actions choose Back up, then save the encrypted file to a location you can reach from your other device. On the new device, choose Use existing key during setup. Choose Restore backup and select the saved file. Restoring replaces the records on that device after confirmation.

Unlock and recovery

Your device needs an enrolled strong biometric. An optional Authenticator entry belongs to the device that enrolled it. Keep the private recovery key separately from the backup. Kylindravia cannot retrieve a lost key.

Contact

For technical support or purchase questions, email kylindravia@gmail.com. Include the app version, operating system and the step where the problem occurred. Keep passwords, recovery keys, codes and signing files private.
