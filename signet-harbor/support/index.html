Signet Harbor Support

Connect a second device

In version 1.4, unlock the device that holds your records. Choose Connect Google Drive, select your Google account and wait for Up to date. On a new device, enter your private key under Use existing key during setup. Open the vault and connect the same Google account. Signet Harbor finds the matching encrypted vault automatically.

Changes made offline

The home screen shows the last verified check and any pending changes. Keep the vault open when reconnecting so it can verify the latest records. If two devices edit the same record differently, both versions appear. Look for Preserved version beside the additional copy.

Restore a portable backup

Choose Back up in Vault actions to export an encrypted file. To recover, enter the same recovery key during device setup, then choose Restore backup and select the file. In a Drive-linked vault, the app merges the records. In a local-only vault, it asks before replacing the local records.

Unlock and recovery

Your device needs an enrolled strong biometric. An optional Authenticator entry belongs to the device that enrolled it. Keep the private recovery key separately from the backup. Kylindravia cannot retrieve a lost key.

Contact

For technical support or purchase questions, email kylindravia@gmail.com. Include the app version, operating system and the step where the problem occurred. Keep passwords, recovery keys, codes and signing files private.
