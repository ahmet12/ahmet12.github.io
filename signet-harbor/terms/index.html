Your backup

Concurrent different versions are preserved automatically. Check the last successful sync, keep an independently accessible recovery copy and test a restore before relying on it. A linked vault merges imported records. A local-only vault asks before replacing its records from an export. Exporting an individual attachment writes the original decrypted file.
