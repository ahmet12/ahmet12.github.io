Signet Harbor

Keep API keys, certificates and signing files in one vault. Organize records by project and unlock them with your device biometrics. You can also require an Authenticator code.

Back up and recover

In version 1.4, connect your own Google Drive to sync encrypted records between Mac and Android. Use the same recovery key and Google account on both devices. Different edits to the same record are saved as separate versions.

You can also export a portable encrypted backup. The backup file and recovery key let you restore a vault offline.
