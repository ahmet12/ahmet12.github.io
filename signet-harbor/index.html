Signet Harbor

Keep API keys, certificates and signing files in one vault. Organize records by project and unlock them with your device biometrics. You can also require an Authenticator code.

Back up and recover

Export an encrypted archive to your chosen location, including Google Drive. To recover on a new device, you need both the archive and the recovery key. Backups are manual.
