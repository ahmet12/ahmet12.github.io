Signet Harbor Privacy Notice

Version 1.2 · 21 November 2026

Who operates Signet Harbor

Ahmet Kılıç, trading as Kylindravia in Türkiye, publishes Signet Harbor. He is the controller for support correspondence, commercial records and privacy requests that he actually receives. Contact kylindravia@gmail.com. The app has no Kylindravia account, vault server, analytics, crash-reporting SDK or advertising identifier.

Your vault and keys

The app processes record titles, notes, filenames and attachments locally to encrypt, display and restore them. Kylindravia receives none of those contents, your private recovery key, Authenticator secret or codes, or biometric templates. The operating system performs biometric verification. The device keeps a locally protected enrollment and an encrypted working vault. The time of a successful one-time code and failed-attempt state are stored locally for replay protection and rate limiting. The device remembers when you finish or skip the quick guide. Acceptance of the terms version is also recorded locally. There is no acceptance registry on a Kylindravia server.

A full backup contains encrypted record data, including the original filenames. You choose the destination through the operating system. If you choose Google Drive, your device and your Drive provider handle the transfer under your account. The provider can see account and transfer information, the outer archive name, its size and timing. Kylindravia receives no Drive credentials, OAuth tokens, files or folder listings. Exporting one decrypted attachment exposes its contents to the destination you select.

You decide whether to save your private key in Google Password Manager, and whether to sync your Authenticator entry. Those services operate under their own settings and terms. Copying a secret makes it available to the system clipboard and potentially to approved clipboard tools. The app marks sensitive copies and requests clearing where supported. Keep decrypted exports and clipboard contents private.

Purchases, sponsors and optional contacts

On Android, the editions screen uses the installed Google Play service to check purchase status and, where offered, subscription pricing. When an active sponsor campaign is bundled with the app, opening the unlocked vault home screen also checks the Play account’s ad-free entitlement. The vault screen receives only a decision about showing the card. The app sends no vault content to Play. The app processes product and purchase status and tokens locally to grant ad-free access and acknowledge completed purchases. It has no Kylindravia purchase server. Google handles checkout and payment details. The publisher may receive order information such as order references, amounts, currency, country, refunds and payout reports through Play Console. The publisher does not receive card numbers from the app.

The free Android edition can display a labelled card from an independent sponsor below the vault home controls. After a successful full backup it can also display an immediately dismissible full-screen sponsor message alongside the save confirmation. This uses the existing local eligibility decision. The app sends no backup event, account, filename or destination to the sponsor or Google Play. A paid subscription or an inconclusive store response hides both placements. Its text and link are bundled with the signed app. Displaying it sends no impression, identifier or vault data to the sponsor. If you choose Visit sponsor, Android opens the sponsor’s public website in the browser or app registered for that link. That site and your browser may process your IP address and other connection data under their own policies. The macOS companion has no advertising or billing integration.

If you download the app from GitHub, GitHub handles the download and authentication for private repositories under its policies. If you email us, our email provider processes your email address, message and any information you choose to include. Never include credentials, recovery keys, one-time codes or backups. We use your message to resolve the request. A privacy request may require proportionate identity verification.

Use, recipients and retention

We handle only the records needed to respond to correspondence, deliver and administer an optional purchase, resolve disputes, comply with applicable accounting duties or respond to lawful requests. The corresponding grounds, where applicable under Turkish Law No. 6698, are performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, establishment or protection of a right, and legitimate interests balanced against your rights. Electronic correspondence and store reports are the collection channels. Recipients can include the relevant email or store provider, advisers assisting with a dispute, and authorities where disclosure is legally required. Google, GitHub or the destination provider may process data outside your country. Your storage choice does not give the publisher access to your account.

Local vault files remain on a device until you remove the relevant app data. Your storage service controls retention and trash for exported copies. Delete those copies separately. Android subscriptions are managed in Google Play. We retain correspondence for the time needed to close the request and handle associated claims. Statutory records are retained for the legally applicable period. We do not sell vault contents or use them to select ads.

Your rights

You may ask whether we process personal data about you, request information about its use and recipients, including transfers abroad, request correction or deletion when the legal conditions are met, ask that relevant recipients be notified, object to an adverse result based solely on automated processing, and seek compensation for unlawful processing. Send a request to kylindravia@gmail.com identifying Signet Harbor and the right you wish to exercise. We respond within the applicable statutory period, generally up to 30 days for a valid request under Turkish law. You retain the right to apply to the relevant supervisory authority. To act on a Drive, Password Manager, Authenticator or Play account, use that provider's account controls.
